OBSID-FINANCE  —  interactive demo
=====================================

This folder contains the Obsid-finance website and the Personal / Business app.
It runs entirely on your own computer. Nothing is installed, nothing is uploaded,
and it is not reachable from any other device.


HOW TO OPEN IT
--------------

1. Unzip the whole folder first (do not run it from inside the zip).

2. WINDOWS  ->  double-click   "START HERE - Windows.bat"
   If Windows shows a blue "protected your PC" box: click "More info", then "Run anyway".

   MAC         ->  double-click   "START HERE - Mac.command"
   If macOS says it cannot be opened:
     a) Open the Terminal app, type   bash    then a space, drag the
        "START HERE - Mac.command" file into the Terminal window, and press Enter.
        (This always works.)
     b) Or: System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > "Open Anyway".

3. A black window appears and your browser opens the demo by itself.
   If it doesn't, copy the address shown in the black window
   (for example http://localhost:8765) into Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.

4. To stop: close the black window.


WHERE TO LOOK
-------------

  Website      The page that opens first. Scroll down, try the Scenario Lab sliders
               and the approval demo. Click "Open the app" to go in.
  App          Switch between "Personal" and "Business" in the left menu (top of the
               screen on a phone). Try AI chat, Cash Flow, Transactions, Approvals.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

  * Needs internet the first time you open it, to load the fonts.
  * Use a recent Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on a laptop or desktop.
  * All names, figures and chat answers are SAMPLE data for design review.
    No AI service is connected yet.
  * Keep the "app" and "launcher" folders next to the START HERE files.
